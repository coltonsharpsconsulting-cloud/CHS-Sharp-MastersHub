So — what are we asking?

We don't have a finished plan, and I won't pretend we do. But the evidence points somewhere specific.

Recommendation 1
1
📊

Audit the pipeline

Disaggregate placement, gifted, and advanced-course data by race, language, and feeder school — find who's sorted toward “abstract” before the first bell.

Recommendation 2
2
🔓

Open the gateway courses

Re-examine physics & calculus placement; pilot defaulting qualified students in rather than making them fight their way in.

Recommendation 3
3
🤝

Rebuild counseling

Opportunity-brokering, not rationing. Add capacity beyond two counselors, and train every advisor to interrupt the default Carmen met.

Recommendation 4
4
🌐

Deficit → asset culture

Professional learning that treats a student's bilingualism as the strength it is — not a problem to be managed.

Recommendation 5
5
🎓

Systematic sponsorship

What we did informally for Carmen — a physics mentor — shouldn't depend on a lucky hallway conversation.

In every chapter, the people who changed these structures were never the administrators — they were the families who refused to accept them.

Carmen had the courage to knock. The data say many never will.

Let's make sure no child has to be that brave just to get a fair shot.
